Rooted in Japanese tradition

The Art of Takumi

Takumi means artisan. Three ideas shape everything that leaves our kitchen and our bar, and each one is a discipline rather than a flourish.

  • クラフト

    Craft

    Handcrafted dishes drawn from Japanese culinary tradition, plated with restraint.

  • 味

    Flavour

    Seasonal produce paired with contemporary technique and quiet confidence.

  • 経験

    Experience

    An intimate dining room, designed around every small detail.

The Takumi Circle

Loyalty, crafted like everything else

Free to join, and built around the details that matter. Priority tables, seasonal tasting invitations, and cocktail credit that renews every quarter.

  • Priority tables Members are offered seatings before they open to the public.
  • Seasonal tastings An invitation each time the kitchen changes the menu.
  • Cocktail credit Credit at the bar that renews at the start of every quarter.
  • One membership No tiers and no points. The same welcome every time you sit down.

Membership is free. We will only email you about your membership, and you can leave at any time.
